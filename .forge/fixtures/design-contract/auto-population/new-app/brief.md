# New app brief

**Application:** Field Notes, a web app used by urban tree inspectors.
**First implementation:** A desktop and phone workspace for recording an inspection and finding the latest inspections by street or tree tag.
**Known constraints:** Inspectors may lose connectivity in the field. The first release supports keyboard and touch. No brand palette, typeface, or approval owner has been supplied.

This is the only product evidence available. Keep proposed behavior bounded to the inspection list and inspection capture flows. Mark offline behavior and visual foundations as assumptions for owner review where the brief does not specify details.
