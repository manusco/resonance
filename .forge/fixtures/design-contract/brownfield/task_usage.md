# Example workspace: task usage snapshot

Synthetic interview summary for this fixture, collected from ten sessions per task family:

- Customer lookup: 8 of 10 sessions used name or status, then opened one record.
- Audit review: 7 of 10 sessions filtered by time and sometimes actor, then opened one event.
- Invoice reconciliation: 9 of 10 sessions combined date, account, region, and payment state across a batch.

The first two task families use a small filter set and inspect one result at a time. Reconciliation depends on keeping several filters available together. Treat these counts as fixture evidence for a recommendation, not production analytics or approval.
