# Content Learning Loop

Use this when organic content performance must inform a decision.

## Decide Before Measuring

Every content metric must answer a decision:

- Keep the theme.
- Cut the theme.
- Repackage the idea.
- Change surface.
- Ask for a stronger asset.
- Escalate to paid, SEO, lifecycle, or sales.

If no decision changes, do not report the number.

## Signal Quality

- Reach: distribution, not proof of value.
- Likes: weak preference signal.
- Saves or bookmarks: stronger usefulness signal.
- Comments: useful only when quality is inspected.
- Shares: audience-to-audience transfer, but often context-light.
- Clicks: active interest, but must be deduped and windowed.
- Replies or DMs: high intent, inspect manually.
- Watch retention: promise match, pacing, and drop-off.
- Follower growth: delayed trust signal, not campaign proof by itself.

## Common Traps

- Small samples: do not over-read one post.
- Outlier bias: one lucky post does not define the strategy.
- Cross-channel comparison: a LinkedIn save and a YouTube retention curve are not the same unit.
- Attribution risk: a post may assist a sale without proving cause.
- False causal claims: "this post drove revenue" needs a model, holdout, or clear source path.
- Survivorship bias: inspect failed posts, not only winners.

## Handoff

The channel owner decides what changes. Analytics says what the evidence can and cannot support.
