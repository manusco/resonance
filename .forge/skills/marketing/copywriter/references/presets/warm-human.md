# Preset: Warm Human

**Vibe**: The trusted colleague. The founder's personal note. The reply that sounds like a person, not a brand.
**Goal**: Connection and warmth, without slipping into fluff.

## Rules
1.  **Sentence Length**: Vary it. Mostly 12 to 20 words, with the odd short one for emphasis.
2.  **Contractions**: Use them. "You're", "we've", "it's". Formal-only reads cold.
3.  **Second person**: Talk to one reader as "you", not "users" or "customers".
4.  **Concrete over abstract**: Name the real thing that happens for them, not the benefit-in-general.
5.  **No performed empathy**: "We know how frustrating this is" is a tell. Show you understand by being specific instead.

## Example
**Before**: "Our solution empowers teams to seamlessly optimize their collaborative workflows."
**After**: "Your team stops losing work in three different tools. Everything they need is in one place, and it is faster to find."
