# Content Quality Gates

Quality follows the page's job, audience, evidence, and distinct value. Word counts, link densities, arbitrary uniqueness percentages, and fixed publishing batches are not quality gates.

## Page-Job Gate

Before publishing, state:

1. the audience and task the page serves;
2. the material questions or decision criteria it must cover;
3. the evidence, experience, data, or utility it contributes;
4. how it differs from existing pages;
5. the next action or route it should enable.

Fail the page when it cannot satisfy that contract, regardless of length.

## Scaled and Location Pages

Scale only pages that carry distinct value. A location, integration, product, template, glossary, or comparison page must contain information specific to that entity and useful without relying on token swaps.

Evidence can include:

- actual availability, constraints, or service details;
- original setup instructions, specifications, data, or tools;
- verified local facts or operational differences;
- a genuine comparison based on inspectable criteria;
- source-backed answers to intent-specific questions.

The number of pages is a planning input, not proof of quality or abuse. Review representative and adversarial samples before expansion. Monitor indexing, duplication, engagement, conversions, errors, and manual actions. Pause when evidence shows the template is failing.

## The Swap Test

Replace the entity name with another plausible entity. If the page still reads as correct and useful without material edits, it probably lacks entity-specific value. Consolidate it, enrich it with verified information, or do not publish it.

## Structure and Voice

- Use the format that best serves the information. Do not add lists, tables, quotes, or images to satisfy a spacing quota.
- Rewrite repetitive templates and mechanical sentence patterns that reduce clarity.
- Keep the voice appropriate to the audience. Do not force tone changes as an attempt to evade content detection.
- Use media only when it adds meaning, proof, instruction, or accessibility.

## Internal Links

- Add a link when it helps discovery, supplies needed context, or advances the reader's next task.
- Use descriptive anchor text.
- Give every intended indexable page a crawlable route from the site architecture.
- Do not add links to meet a density or page-type quota.

## Evidence and Schema

- Support material factual claims with primary or best available sources.
- Keep publication and modified dates truthful.
- Recommend structured data only when the visible content and page type meet current eligibility in `schema_types_current.md`.
- Do not promise rankings, rich results, or AI citations from content structure or schema.

## Progressive Rollout

Choose rollout size from reversibility, review capacity, crawl behavior, business risk, and the time needed to observe useful signals. Define stop conditions before expansion. A universal batch size or daily publishing cap is not evidence-based.
