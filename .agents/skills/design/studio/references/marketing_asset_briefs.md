# Marketing Asset Briefs

Use this when Studio creates thumbnails, social graphics, paid or organic creative, newsletter hero images, or campaign visuals.

## Required Brief

- Channel and placement: where the asset appears.
- Job: what the asset must help the audience understand or do.
- Audience promise: the idea the channel owner is distributing.
- Aspect ratio and safe zones: include platform UI, captions, and crop risks.
- Brand constraints: colors, typography notes, style boundaries, and exclusions.
- Copy policy: exact text, placeholder text, or no rendered text.
- Rights and consent: people, logos, screenshots, footage, music, and trademark constraints.
- Accessibility: contrast, text size, alt text notes, and whether the meaning survives without color.

## Thumbnail And Cover Checks

- One focal point.
- Clear contrast between subject and background.
- Text is either absent or large enough to read after compression.
- The face, object, or result supports the promise.
- Nothing important sits under platform UI or crop zones.
- The visual does not imply proof the product cannot support.

## Rejection Rules

Reject prompts that ask for:

- Final small text rendered by an image model.
- Unauthorized likenesses, logos, private screenshots, or customer material.
- Fake before/after proof.
- Visuals that make regulated or safety claims without verified support.
- Crops that hide required disclosure, consent, or context.
